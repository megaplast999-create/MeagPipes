at ₹22,689 and ₹24,109 across all 5 stages.

However, price instability occurs across 5 distinct vulnerability vectors:
1. Cross-Module Discrepancy (Option 1 vs Dynamic Staged Costing):
   - In PILOT_COPY.xlsm (Option 1): Line 1 = ₹22,689/m, Line 2 = ₹24,109/m.
   - In DEV.xlsm (Staged Costing): Line 1 = ₹22,591/m (-₹98), Line 2 = ₹24,005/m (-₹104).
   - In Approved Unipro CostSheet: Line 1 = ₹22,351/m (-₹338), Line 2 = ₹23,570/m (-₹539).
   - In Dynamic Landed FC: Line 1 = ₹22,350/m (-₹339), Line 2 = ₹23,568/m (-₹541).

2. Pilot Table Schema Truncation (24 vs 38 columns):
   - PILOT_COPY.xlsm lacks columns 25-38 (ManualFC, SteelRate, ManualSellingPrice, etc.).
   - Manual selling price overrides cannot be stored in PILOT_COPY tblQuoteLines.
   - Recalculating in PILOT_COPY immediately reverts any overridden price to formula price.

3. OnWorkbookOpen Form Wiping:
   - SetupQuoteSheetUI executes ws.Cells.Clear on QUOTE sheet.
   - Wipes B4:B17 and D4:E14. Recalculation without reloading defaults to empty inputs.

4. modLog.LoadQuote Cell Offset Shift:
   - Writes DeliveryPlace to B11 instead of B10.
   - Writes PlantID to B14 instead of B13.
   - Writes PaymentTerms to B15 instead of B14.
   - Writes ValidityDays to B16 instead of B15.
   - Writes PriceBasis to B17 instead of B16 (corrupting PricingMode in B17).

5. LegacyMode Unrestored:
